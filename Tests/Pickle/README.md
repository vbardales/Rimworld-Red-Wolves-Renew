# Red Wolves Renew Pickle suite

Development only. Nothing under `Tests/` is part of the Workshop payload. The suite is written for the shared WSL runner. **It has been written and checked offline, and never played**: no scenario here claims an in-game result. `STATUS.md` says so too.

The animal and def steps are Nelim's Pickle Tools' (ColonistRace and DefFieldSteps, PickleTools 813dc0f); Virginie ruled on 2026-11-03 that this suite does not copy the Dalmatians Renew steps. Only what they do not cover is local, prefix "Red Wolves Renew": operations compared across animals, stat values, the pass language, a description start, the puppy life stage, and the gender switches resolved for a male, a female and a neutral colonist. Catalogue of the local steps: `Docs/steps.md`, generated by `scripts/Generate-Steps.ps1 -ModRoot RedWolvesRenew`.

## Scope

The offline suite, `Tests/test_mod.py`, owns everything that can be proved without a game: the XML, the values as written, the texture paths, About and LoadFolders, and the real patch files of A Dog Said 2, Dogs mate, Better Crossbreeding and Combat Extended applied to fixtures with the target present and absent.

Pickle is limited to what needs a running game: the values the game kept after loading a whole mod list, the operations an animal is actually offered, the crossing lists other mods build at startup, an information card, the text in the language a pass was launched in, and the `{PAWN_gender ? ... : ... : ...}` switches resolved for a male, a female and a neutral colonist. A scenario that only repeats an offline assertion was not written.

Not written, on purpose: the coats (four textures and a ratio, owner's decision), butchering and rot (the red wolf takes its meat from the timber wolf and has no leather of its own), cameras and captures, the removal chain (a content mod, scenario J of `TESTING.md`), Combat Extended and Animal Gear (offline only; the Animal Gear id matches no installed mod). A pregnancy through to a birth under Better Crossbreeding is not played either.

## Passes

Seven passes, nine launches. Optional mods are staged in the order the mod declares, and the order is the order of the lines in the map: the staging script places every overlay mod before the mod under test unless a line for the mod itself stands among them, which is why every map but the first names `nelim.redwolves`.

| Pass | Map | Features | Language | What it establishes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Without optional mods | `wsl-deps.sans-facultatifs.map` | `01` | English, then French | The mod stands alone: values, biomes, taming, name, trainings, information card, text |
| 2. Pawnmorpher | `wsl-deps.avec-pawnmorpher.map` | `02` | English | The folder loads and every def of it exists (one scenario; the colony-bound ones are cut, see below) |
| 3. A Dog Said 2, declared order | `wsl-deps.avec-ads2.map` | `03` | English | The red wolf is offered the timber wolf's operations |
| 5. Dogs mate | `wsl-deps.avec-dogsmate.map` | `05` | English | The red wolf is in the Wolf group: it seeks, and is sought by, the vanilla wolves |
| 6. Better Crossbreeding | `wsl-deps.avec-bettercrossbreeding.map` | `06` | English | The two halves, list on the male and extension on the mother, both ways, Random |
| 7. Both crossing mods | `wsl-deps.avec-croisements.map` | `05,06,!@without-dogsmate` | English | The two mods write the same list: nothing listed twice or replaced |

From the collection root, once `scripts/Pickle-Status.ps1` says the machine is free:

```powershell
powershell.exe -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/Run-PickleWsl.ps1 -Mod RedWolvesRenew -DepMap wsl-deps.sans-facultatifs.map -Filter '01-standalone,!@french'  -Language English
powershell.exe -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/Run-PickleWsl.ps1 -Mod RedWolvesRenew -DepMap wsl-deps.sans-facultatifs.map -Filter '01-standalone,!@english' -Language French
powershell.exe -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/Run-PickleWsl.ps1 -Mod RedWolvesRenew -DepMap wsl-deps.avec-pawnmorpher.map -Filter '02-pawnmorpher' -Language English
powershell.exe -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/Run-PickleWsl.ps1 -Mod RedWolvesRenew -DepMap wsl-deps.avec-ads2.map -Filter '03-ads2' -Language English
powershell.exe -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/Run-PickleWsl.ps1 -Mod RedWolvesRenew -DepMap wsl-deps.avec-dogsmate.map -Filter '05-dogsmate' -Language English
powershell.exe -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/Run-PickleWsl.ps1 -Mod RedWolvesRenew -DepMap wsl-deps.avec-bettercrossbreeding.map -Filter '06-bettercrossbreeding' -Language English
powershell.exe -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/Run-PickleWsl.ps1 -Mod RedWolvesRenew -DepMap wsl-deps.avec-croisements.map -Filter '05-dogsmate,06-bettercrossbreeding,!@without-dogsmate' -Language English
```

In practice a ticket is filed through the Ticket Manager (`file-pickle-ticket`), not by running the script by hand. Do not run the Windows game, do not stage by hand, and do not switch language inside a scenario.

## Preconditions that are not in this repository

- Staged beside the mod in every pass: `nelim.pickletools.colonistrace` and `nelim.pickletools.deffields` (PickleTools, steps compiled by NPT, not played until a ticket is green; NPT says when).
- Workshop copies of Humanoid Alien Races (`839005762`), Pawnmorpher (`1786466855`), A Dog Said Animal Prosthetics 2 (`3238353862`), Dogs mate (`2441132298`) and Better Crossbreeding (`3520675842`) are in the Windows Workshop folder (checked 2026-11-03).
- The scenarios use the `test-colony` fixture that ships with Pickle, and need at least one free colonist.
- Pawnmorpher's hard dependency list for this game version is Humanoid Alien Races only (`modDependenciesByVersion`, `v1.6`); if the first Pawnmorpher run reports a missing mod, add it to the map and record why.
- Pawnmorpher may log errors of its own: if pass 2 fails on `no errors were logged`, read whose they are before reading anything into it.

## Tags

- `@english` and `@french`: written against the language of the pass; the filter keeps the other out.
- `@requires:<packageId>`: the feature needs that mod; the pass map stages it.
- `@without-dogsmate`: a scenario that holds only when Dogs mate is absent; pass 7 excludes it.

## Build

`Tests/Pickle/Source` builds `Mod/Pickle/Assemblies/RedWolvesRenew.PickleSteps.dll` (net48, Krafs reference packages, Pickle's reference package): `dotnet build Tests/Pickle/Source/RedWolvesRenew.PickleSteps.csproj -c Release`. The intermediate output goes to `build/pickle/` (ignored). The assembly is committed, as in the Dalmatians Renew suite, because the runner stages `Tests/Pickle/Mod` as it stands. Check the steps against the features with `scripts/Check-Steps.ps1 -Mod RedWolvesRenew -StepPrefix "Red Wolves Renew"` before any ticket.
